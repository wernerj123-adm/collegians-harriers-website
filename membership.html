New-member essentials.

Once-off items are reflected in the published new-member schedule.

01

Access card

First-time Senior, Senior 70+ and Student members require a Collegians Club access card. The published once-off charge is R55; Juniors are excluded.

02

Club kit contribution

The published discounted once-off T-shirt or vest contribution is R100 for Senior, Senior 70+ and Student members and R50 for Juniors.

03

Family discount

Senior members joining as a family receive a R25 discount per Senior, up to a maximum of two Senior family members**Discount to be provided at Tuesday Time Trial
