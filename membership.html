Membership information, current fees and the essentials you need to wear the red, black and white.

Existing members

2026 total subscriptions including KZN Athletics are R790 for Seniors, R595 for Seniors 70+, R480 for Students, R245 for Juniors 12–19 and R190 for Juniors under 12.

New members

2026 totals are R945 for Seniors, R750 for Seniors 70+, R635 for Students, R295 for Juniors 12–19 and R240 for Juniors under 12. The published fee schedule includes the new-member items described below.

Family discount

Senior members joining as a family receive a R25 discount per Senior, up to a maximum of two Senior family members.

Access card

First-time members are required to purchase a Collegians Club access card. The published once-off charge is R55 for Seniors, Seniors 70+ and Students; Juniors are excluded.

New-member kit

The published discounted once-off T-shirt/vest contribution is R100 for Seniors, Seniors 70+ and Students and R50 for Juniors.
