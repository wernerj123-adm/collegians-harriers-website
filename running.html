There is more than one way to run Collegians.

The Running section is the home for regular club activity: time trials, speed and interval work, road running, trail running, club championship information and the practical resources members use throughout the season.

As we finish the production site, current weekly times and route details will be presented here in one easy-to-update schedule rather than being scattered across separate pages.
