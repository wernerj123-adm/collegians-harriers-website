SATURDAY · 17 OCTOBER 2026 · 06:00–18:00

The Longest
Day.

How far can you go? A unique 12-hour endurance challenge for runners and walkers on the grassed sportsfield at Collegians Club, 381 Boshoff Street, Pietermaritzburg.

Solo entrants

Minimum age 20 years. Runners must complete at least 80 km and walkers at least 60 km to be official finishers.

3-person teams

Minimum age 19 years. Team runners may rotate and complete any distance during their turns; runner teams require at least 80 km and walker teams at least 60 km.

4-person teams

Minimum age 14 years. The 2026 programme includes dedicated Schools and Over-60s team categories.

NEW FOR 2026

Schools Team

Teams of four, for schools only. The competition has a maximum of 80 km: the first Schools Team to reach 80 km, by fastest recorded time, is the winner. Distance after 80 km does not count for this competition.

NEW FOR 2026

Over-60s Team

Teams of four, over-60s only. Runner teams must complete at least 80 km and walker teams at least 60 km during the 12-hour event, with ranking by total distance covered.

ENTRIES

R450 – R1,160

Entries are limited to 100 runners and 50 walkers, with a team regarded as one entry. The supplied 2026 page states that entries are via the Webtickets portal.

EVENT DAY

12 hours. One challenge.

Walkers use the inside lane and runners the outside lane, and participants must remain in their allocated lane. Race-pack collection is scheduled for 05:00–05:40 in the Collegians Club Hall, followed by the pre-race briefing.
