OUR STORY

From Maritzburg Harriers
to Collegians.

Collegians Harriers began on 24 January 1933 as the Maritzburg Harriers Athletic Club, a small track club whose early stars included Natal 880 yards champion Bert Bendzulla and SA 1000 yards record holder Skonk Nicholson. In 1950 it became part of the newly formed Collegians Club and took the name it carries today.

For 34 years, from 1948 to 1981, the club organised the Comrades Marathon, opening it to all races and to women in 1975, before handing it to the Comrades Marathon Association. Since then it has hosted its own races, from the Tuesday Herman's Delight to The Longest Day and the uMngeni-uThukela Water Marathon.

Club greatsBendzulla · Nicholson · Van der Leeuw · Piper · Baker · Vorster

Piet van der Leeuw set an SA 880 yards record in 1956; Gordon Baker won eight Comrades golds; Piet Vorster won the 1979 Comrades.

Club
timeline.

The milestones that shaped the club, from its founding meeting to today.

1933

Maritzburg Harriers founded

24 January: inaugural meeting in the Natal Education Department building, Longmarket Street, chaired by C.E. "Sax" Young. Bert Bendzulla is first captain and secretary; Dr H.H. Croudace first president.

1937

Gunga Din team trophy

Harriers win the Comrades team trophy, won again in 1950 and 1951.

1948

Comrades organisers

The club takes over the Comrades Marathon and builds it up over the next 34 years.

1950

Collegians Harriers

24 January: the club becomes a sub-section of the newly formed Collegians Club and takes its present name.

1958

Collegians Club finish

Ten Comrades up runs finish at the Collegians Club grounds between 1958 and 1975.

1960

Herman's Delight

26 July: Herman Delvin starts the weekly time trial, held every week since.

1975

Comrades opens to all

Under the club's organisation, Comrades becomes the first major South African sporting event open to all races and to women.

1979

Piet Vorster wins Comrades

5:45:02, a new up-run record, by a Collegians runner in a race the club organised.

1981

The CMA is formed

A Special General Meeting on 23 September hands Comrades to a new Comrades Marathon Association.

1995

Back to the mother club

1 February: the club re-joins the Collegians Club.

1996

The Longest Day

9 August: Brian Swart and Theo Millar's 12-hour track race is run for the first time.

1999

Umgeni Water Marathon

First edition of the marathon at Midmar Dam with Howick AC, now the uMngeni-uThukela Water Marathon.

2026

A new chapter

The club portal and this website are built, carrying the club's results and history online.
