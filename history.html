Tell the story.

The retained club history records the origins of the Maritzburg Harriers Athletic Club, the people who shaped Collegians Harriers, and the races the club organised across generations.

These downloads remain source documents rather than rewritten summaries. That protects names, dates and historical context while new research can be added in future versions.
