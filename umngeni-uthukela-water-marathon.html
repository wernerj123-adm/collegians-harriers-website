2026 RACE INFORMATION

Choose the distance.
Share the water.

The 2026 event starts and finishes at the main slipway inside Midmar Dam Resort. The 5 km and 10 km routes remain inside the resort; the longer routes include approximately 4 km on public roads before returning to the reserve.

Online entries are available through Webtickets and participating Pick n Pay and Boxer stores. Entry limits are 1,000 athletes across the 10 km, 21.1 km and 42.2 km, plus 400 participants in the 5 km fun run.

ImportantNo temporary licence for the marathon

Temporary licences are available for the 10 km and half marathon. Minimum ages and all race rules appear in the official flyer below.
